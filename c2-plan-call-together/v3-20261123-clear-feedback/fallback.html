Known customer case

You and Lucy are preparing for a joint call with Amira. The meeting is scheduled for tomorrow morning, and your goal is to help her explore how cloud capabilities could improve operational efficiency and order fulfillment.

Before you meet, you and Lucy decide to review what you know about the customer. Dawnstar Diagnostics is a mid-sized healthcare company that distributes medical equipment to hospitals and clinics. The company is growing quickly, but Amira’s team is struggling with outdated inventory systems, inconsistent data, and delayed deliveries.

Amira is eager to modernize operations, but she wants to see clear business value before committing to new technology. She is analytical and results-focused but also cautious about adopting large-scale solutions without proven outcomes.

You lead the business conversation; Lucy is your solutions architect and contributes technical assessment. Amira contributes the customer’s goals, constraints and operational requirements. Agree how you will work together rather than treating those roles as exclusive speaking rights.

Amira's question for the practice response

This sounds promising, but our team doesn’t have much experience managing data systems. How would this actually work for us day to day?
