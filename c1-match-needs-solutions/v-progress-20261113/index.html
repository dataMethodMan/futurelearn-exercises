        From customer concern to cloud capability - complete text version

        Your success in a sales team depends on listening carefully to what customers say about their business challenges, and then connecting those needs to the right cloud capability. In this activity you are an entry-level sales associate preparing for discovery calls. You will study a reference sheet of common concerns, then work through four customer scenarios: identify the business need, choose the cloud capability that answers it and explain your reasoning to the customer. You will finish by recalling the pairings without the sheet.

        For each scenario, first identify the customer’s business need, then choose the cloud capability that best addresses it, then write a short explanation in your own words and compare it with a model answer. If a choice is not right, the feedback explains why and you can try again.

        The businesses are fictional. This activity is not scored. Your work and checked attempts are saved automatically in this browser. Return here to resume; Restart clears saved work and attempts for this activity. Nothing is submitted. Allow about 20 minutes.

        You can do every stage on paper or in a document.         Cloud solutions reference sheet

        Common client concerns, the business needs behind them, and the cloud capabilities that address them. Use the sheet to guide your answers.

        Client concern: “Our systems can’t handle peak demand.” Business need: Limited scalability. Cloud capability: Elastic computing and auto-scaling.

        Client concern: “We’re worried about protecting customer data.” Business need: Data security concerns. Cloud capability: Encryption, identity management and compliance controls.

        Client concern: “We’re spending too much on maintaining servers.” Business need: High infrastructure costs. Cloud capability: Pay-as-you-go pricing and resource optimisation.

        Client concern: “It takes us months to launch new products.” Business need: Slow time to market. Cloud capability: Automated deployment and development and operations, or DevOps, integration.

        Client concern: “If our system goes down, we could lose everything.” Business need: Lack of data backup or disaster recovery. Cloud capability: Cloud storage redundancy and disaster recovery services.

        Scenario 1 of 4: A growing logistics company

        A small logistics company has recently secured several new contracts and expects its business volume to double within the next year. However, its existing technology systems are already struggling to keep up, and it has reached out to your organisation for help. During a discovery call with the leadership team, one manager says: “We need a solution that can grow with us without constant investment in new servers or hardware.”

        Model answer: Elastic computing and auto-scaling are ideal cloud capabilities for this challenge.
