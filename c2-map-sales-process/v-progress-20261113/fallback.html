Sales stage reference

Prospect: Create initial interest and explore high-level relevance.

Qualify: Check whether need, decision process and timing justify further work.

Discover: Investigate detailed requirements, challenges and desired outcomes.

Propose: Discuss a concrete solution, pricing and implementation path before a purchase commitment.

Close: Finalise terms and secure a purchase commitment.

Nurture: Support, renew or expand an existing customer relationship after purchase.
