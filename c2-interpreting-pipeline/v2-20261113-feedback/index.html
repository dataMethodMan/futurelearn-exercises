        Interpreting the pipeline

        Complete text version

        Inspect a fictional CRM snapshot and build a short pipeline review. Separate active opportunities from closed outcomes, compare time in stage, then use deal values and expected close dates to explain one strength and one risk.

        This is ungraded practice. The checks compare your selections and counts with the displayed data; your optional written notes are for your own reflection. Nothing is submitted or saved after reloading. Download your review if you want to keep it.

        Definitions and snapshot

        The snapshot is fixed at 19 August 2025. Days in stage and expected close dates belong to that fictional snapshot.

        Prospect, Qualify, Discover, Propose and Close describe active sales work. Nurture describes work with an existing customer. Closed Won and Closed Lost are recorded outcomes. This snapshot has no active records labelled Propose, Close or Nurture.

        Compare time in stage with peers at the same stage. An outlier is a reason to investigate, not proof of a stalled or lost deal. No stage-duration target is supplied.

        A snapshot shows current distribution. It does not show how many deals moved between stages over a period, so it cannot establish a conversion rate or steady progression.

        Deal values use a common monetary unit. An expected close date is an estimate; potential deal value is not guaranteed revenue. Exclude closed outcomes when reviewing active opportunities.
