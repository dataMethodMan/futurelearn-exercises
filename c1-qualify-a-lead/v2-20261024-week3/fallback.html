Qualify three leads - complete text version

You have just come back from a busy industry event where you met dozens of potential customers. Several have since followed up to say they are interested in your company’s cloud solutions. Your manager has asked you to run a quick assessment of three leads, using the Challenges, Authority, Money and Prioritization (CHAMP) model, so that you can decide which to focus on first. You do not have all the details, and some of what you have is partial or vague. That is how qualification usually starts.

For each lead, rate each part of the CHAMP model, decide whether the lead is worth pursuing and choose a follow-up question that would close the biggest gap. Then decide which lead to contact first and which to treat lightly. If a choice is not right, the feedback explains why and you can try again. There is no perfect lead. The aim is to understand fit and readiness.

The companies and people are fictional. This activity is not scored, and nothing you choose or write is stored or sent anywhere unless you save a text file yourself. Allow about 15 minutes.

How to use this version

You can do every stage on paper or in a document. For each lead, read the profile, then rate Challenges, Authority, Money and Prioritization as a strong signal, weak or uncertain, or missing. Decide whether the lead is worth pursuing and choose the follow-up question you think would close the biggest gap. Then read the feedback for every option and compare it with your choices. If you chose differently, work out why before moving on. After the three leads, choose which to contact first and which to treat lightly.

The CHAMP model

CHAMP helps you judge fit. For each lead, ask four questions.

Challenges: Is there a problem you can solve?

Authority: Can this person make or shape the decision?

Money: Is budget available or likely?

Prioritization: Is there urgency or a timeline?

She mentioned that budgets are tight this year but may open up next quarter.
