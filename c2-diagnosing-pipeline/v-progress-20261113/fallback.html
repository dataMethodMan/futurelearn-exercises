Definitions and example

Report A is the supplied healthy example for this exercise. Its records have 3, 5, 7, 6 and 4 days in their respective stages; close probability estimates rise from 20% at Prospect to 90% at Close.

Each report contains one opportunity in each of five work stages. Counts therefore do not establish a large backlog, measured conversion rate or movement over time. Compare the supplied time-in-stage and probability patterns instead.

Use A as a comparison, not a universal stage-duration target. An ageing difference suggests a question to investigate; the report does not establish its cause or prove an opportunity is lost.

Close probability is the supplied estimate for each opportunity, not a measured conversion rate. Forecasted revenue here is deal value multiplied by that estimate; it is not guaranteed revenue. Values use a common monetary unit.

No sales target or reporting period is supplied. The reports do not establish target coverage or realised sales performance. A Close work stage is distinct from an outcome such as Closed Won.
