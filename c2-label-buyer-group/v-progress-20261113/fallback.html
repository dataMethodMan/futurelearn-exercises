Security upgrade at Solitude Innovations

You are a sales representative for Anarres Systems, a cybersecurity startup that serves emerging tech startups. Solitude Innovations builds artificial intelligence (AI) analytics tools and is preparing a new release for larger customers. It needs stronger security and audit readiness. Your company offers a platform with threat monitoring and access controls that can be embedded into their system. The timeline is tight, and the budget is under review. Map the stakeholders, their goals and concerns, and the order in which you would engage them.

Guidance for the map

Decision-maker: approves major changes in this case. Influencer: shapes the plan or selection. Blocker: can delay progress if risk, governance or commercial conditions are unresolved. End user: works with the tools and experiences their daily impact.

These are primary roles for this exercise, not fixed personality types. A stakeholder may also influence others. Addressing a governance concern is part of building a sound plan.

Influence is the ability to approve, stop or change a decision. Interest is how much the decision affects someone's goals or daily work. Use High, Medium or Low and explain your judgement.

An engagement order is a proposed sequence of purposeful conversations, not a rule to withhold information from people until their turn. You may need an early sponsor check and a later approval discussion with the same person. This map records one main conversation per stakeholder.
