Who is buying and why?

Explore five fictional practice cases. Open records in any order, make or revise a tentative match, then choose the evidence that supports your final match. No score or timer. Your choices last only while this page is open.

B2B means a business buying from another business for an organisational purpose. B2C means an individual buying from a business for personal use. Product, price and pace alone do not define the sales structure.

If you compare without choosing both an opened record and a sales structure: Open a record and select it as your evidence, then choose B2B or B2C before comparing your reasoning.

Compare the cases

Look at who is buying and what the purchase is for. The same shoes were B2C for Jo and B2B for the gym company. The quick café order was B2B; the expensive bicycle chosen over several weeks was B2C. The cloud software supported company operations and was B2B. Speed and price can help describe a sale, but they do not define its structure.
