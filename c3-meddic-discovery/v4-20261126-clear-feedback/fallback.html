Qualify an opportunity with MEDDIC

Fictional teaching case: Harbourline Parts, HP-027. Use the evidence available at each date. This text/PDF alternative contains all prompts, options and feedback. Work on paper or in a document. Offline answers are not automatically checked or saved.

Your task

At each stage, rate six MEDDIC entries, choose a next action and follow-up question. Then prioritise effort and write a CRM note. Work through each stage before reading the next stage or worked feedback. The complete activity takes about 25–30 minutes.

MEDDIC guide

- Metrics: the measurable benefit the customer could gain.

- Economic Buyer: the person with decision authority over the funds.

- Decision Criteria: the requirements used to judge options.

- Decision Process: the steps and people involved in the buying decision.

- Identify Pain: the customer problem and its consequences.

- Champion: influential internal advocacy that helps a proposal progress.

Evidence status guide

- Known: The stated evidence supports this scoped entry. Other parts of the opportunity may remain open.

- Partial: There is useful evidence, but a material part of this entry remains uncertain or untested.

- Missing: The evidence does not establish this element yet. This is a gap to investigate, not a negative verdict.

- Contradicted: New evidence overturns a previous entry. Preserve its history and replace the current claim.

Excel and saving

The interactive practice offers a separate learner Excel at each stage with only evidence available to that date. Amber cells are for your own entries. Worked Excel answers are a separate download after completion. The shared original learning CRM and previous worked workbook are preserved in the earlier edition. Offline Excel work does not import into the browser activity.

The browser activity automatically saves unfinished work and past checked attempts in this browser and device when storage is available. Saved work provides history and JSON backup/restore. Restart clears only this activity after confirmation, including previous-edition work and attempts. Cancel keeps it. Exports remain yours. If saving is blocked or full, download a backup before leaving.

Previous matching answers and notes are retained when returning from the earlier edition. New decisions must be checked afresh; earlier completion is not proof of the revised task.
